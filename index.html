THE GHORERPUSHTI APPROACH

Food shopping should feel simple, clear and considered.

GhorerPushti is a food e-commerce concept focused on helping households explore everyday essentials in one convenient place. We aim to make product information easier to understand and shopping easier to navigate.

01 / Clear information
Product names, prices and pack sizes are easy to find.

02 / A thoughtful selection
A pantry-focused range for everyday needs.

03 / A better experience
A clean, user-friendly path from browsing to checkout.

HOW THE BUSINESS WORKS

From product to doorstep.

01

Business category

Business-to-consumer (B2C): the store presents products directly to household customers.

02

Revenue model

Direct product sales are the main proposed revenue source. Delivery fees may be charged where applicable.

03

Potential partners

Potential Bangladesh partners include bKash, Nagad, SSLCOMMERZ and courier providers such as Pathao Courier or Steadfast. Partnership is not claimed as confirmed.

THE BUSINESS MODEL

GhorerPushti at a glance.

A proposed business model canvas for the project.

Key partners

Food suppliers, farmers, packaging providers, payment services and couriers.

Key activities

Product sourcing, quality checks, listing products, order handling and customer support.

Value propositions

Convenient browsing, clear product details, a pantry-focused range and an easy ordering journey.

Customer relationships

Helpful support, clear order updates, feedback and repeat-customer offers.

Customer segments

Households and individual customers seeking everyday food essentials.

Key resources

Brand, product information, supplier network, website and order-handling process.

Channels

Website, social media pages, customer messaging and delivery services.

Cost structure

Product purchasing, packaging, delivery, payment fees, marketing and website operations.

Revenue streams

Direct product sales and, where applicable, delivery charges or curated bundles.
