THE GHORERPUSHTI APPROACH

A better food-shopping experience begins with trust.

At GhorerPushti.com, we believe choosing everyday food should be simple, informed and convenient. Our goal is to bring a thoughtfully selected range of food essentials together in one place, supported by clear product information, transparent pricing and a customer-first shopping experience.

We aim to build responsible relationships with dependable suppliers and rural producers, making it easier for households to discover products and understand what they are buying.

As we grow, our focus will remain on thoughtful selection, honest communication and reliable service—helping families make informed choices for their everyday needs.

01 / Product transparency
Clear prices, pack sizes and honest product information.

02 / Thoughtful sourcing
Carefully selected everyday foods from dependable suppliers.

03 / Customer-first service
Convenient shopping, clear communication and dependable support.

HOW THE BUSINESS WORKS

From product to doorstep.

01

Business category

Business-to-consumer (B2C): the store presents products directly to household customers.

02

Revenue model

Direct product sales are the main proposed revenue source. Delivery fees may be charged where applicable.

03

Potential partners

Potential Bangladesh partners include bKash, Nagad, SSLCOMMERZ and courier providers such as Pathao Courier or Steadfast. Partnership is not claimed as confirmed.

THE BUSINESS MODEL

Key partners
